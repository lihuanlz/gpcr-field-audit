# Note on pandas
# -------------
# The Atlas pipeline builds its assay-group key by string concatenation. Under pandas 1.x a
# missing component stringified as "nan"; pandas 2+ propagate the missing value, the key
# itself becomes missing, groupby drops the row and the same input collapses from 269 groups
# and 17,987 pairs to 132 groups and 10,864 pairs. code67/71/73/77 now pin the 1.x behaviour
# with an explicit helper, so the archived tables re-derive exactly under the versions below.
# `run_all.py --check` enforces this: the Atlas tables are checked strictly, and only the P5
# admission step is treated as a known environment difference.
#
# Note on P5 (Keshelava 2018, SI section 2)
# -----------------------------------------
# The archived P5 tables come from the 2026-08-16 run, which admitted 11 experiments and
# 208 cells (195 after the erratum exclusions). Under the pinned versions below the
# experiment-admission step of code47 admits 12 experiments and 233 cells (218 after the
# erratum): three of 27 borderline experiments cross the chain-validation threshold. The
# unit AUCs move by at most 0.03 (robust arms 0.13 and 0.23) and every adjudication clause
# reproduces verbatim. The admission step is the numerically sensitive part; the verdicts
# are not. `run_all.py --check` marks these files as known environment differences.
